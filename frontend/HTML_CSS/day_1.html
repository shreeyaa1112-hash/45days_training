The Future of Artificial Intelligence in Everyday Life

Introduction

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is one of the most transformative technologies of the 21st century. From virtual assistants to self-driving cars, AI is changing how people work, communicate, and solve problems. As technology continues to evolve, AI is becoming an essential part of our daily lives.

What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence refers to the ability of machines and computer systems to perform tasks that typically require human intelligence. These tasks include learning, reasoning, problem-solving, language understanding, and decision-making.
